About Us

Nasrallah Bake House is a cherished bakery in Zahle Rassieh, Lebanon, specializing in fresh pastries, artisan breads, and delicious breakfast items. With over 10 years of experience, we pride ourselves on using the finest ingredients and traditional baking methods.

Our bakery is open daily, serving warm, fresh baked goods to our loyal customers. Whether you're looking for morning pastries or specialty breads, we've got you covered!
